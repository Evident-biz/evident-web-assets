      Dual Price & Cash Discount Explained

      Two simple ways to keep your prices fair

      Both of these do the exact same thing: they help cover the card processing fee without you losing money. Here's how each one looks to your customer.

      Pay as little as 0% on processing

      This isn't a workaround, it's one of the best programs available for a business that takes cards. Done right, it can bring your effective processing cost down to zero.

      ✓More transparent than surcharging. Surcharge programs tack on a fee at checkout that can feel like a surprise. Dual Pricing and Cash Discounting show the real price up front, nothing hidden, nothing added at the register.
      ✓Covers all cards, not just credit. Surcharge programs only apply to credit cards, but credit is often less than half of a business's card volume, debit usually makes up the bigger share. Dual Pricing and Cash Discounting apply to every card, so you're covered on the transactions that actually make up most of your day.
      Dual Pricing, Two Prices, Clear Choice
      Cash Price$5.00
      Card Price$5.20

      The menu or sign shows both prices, side by side. The customer sees both and simply picks how they want to pay.

      Cash Discount, One Price, Cash Saves
      Your Price$5.20

      💵 Pay with cash and save $0.20!

      The menu shows one price. Customers who pay with cash get knocked back down to $5.00 automatically.

      Let's break it down with real numbers

      Say an item costs $5.00. Paying with a card costs the business 3–5% in fees, so the menu price is bumped up a little to $5.20. If the customer pays cash, that little bit gets taken right back off, so cash customers still pay $5.00.

      It works the same way for a whole day of sales. Say a business does $100 in card sales in a day:

      $104collected from customers
      →
      $4goes to the processor (the fee)
      →
      $100lands in the bank

      The account is automatically set up to split it this way every single day, the business ends up with the same profit whether the customer paid with cash or card.

      ✅

      Dual Pricing is 100% legal and compliant in all 50 states. Cash Discounting is legal everywhere too, except New York, where it's currently not allowed. Dual Pricing is the safe choice for businesses with New York locations.
